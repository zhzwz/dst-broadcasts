[h1]Broadcasts｜永恒电台[/h1]

为《饥荒联机版》服务器提供及时、清晰的世界事件播报。
Timely and clear world event announcements for Don't Starve Together servers.

[b]纯服务端模组[/b]，客户端无需订阅，不会向存档写入额外数据。
[b]Server-only mod.[/b] Clients do not need to subscribe, and no extra data is written to saves.

[h2]功能｜Features[/h2]

[h3]永恒早报｜The Constant Daily[/h3]
每天开始时报告世界日期、季节、天气、事件、巨兽等。
Reports the world day, season, weather, events, bosses, and more at the start of each day.

[h3]袭击预警｜Attack Warnings[/h3]
在猎犬、洞穴蠕虫、巨鹿、熊獾来袭前的多个时间节点提醒。
Provides countdown warnings for Hound, Depths Worm, Deerclops, and Bearger attacks.

[h3]世界事件｜World Events[/h3]
播报地表与洞穴的重要动态，包括季节与天气趋势、噩梦循环、洞穴地震、酸雨及远古遗迹重置。
Announces major surface and cave events, including seasonal and weather trends, nightmare cycles, earthquakes, acid rain, and Ruins resets.

[h3]物品状态｜Item Status[/h3]
提醒玩家持有物品的低耐久、低燃料与损毁状态。
Warns players about low durability, low fuel, and broken items they are carrying.

[h3]巨兽动态｜Boss Updates[/h3]
巨兽现身或被击败时立即通知当前分片的所有玩家。
Notifies all players on the current shard when a boss appears or is defeated.
